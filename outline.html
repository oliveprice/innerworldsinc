01
Client Discovery

Schedule a meeting with the client, AZ Mountain Inn, to establish shared understanding before any work begins. This conversation anchors all future decisions.

—
What are you trying to say or do?
—
Who are your competitors?
—
How are you different?
—
Why should a visitor care?
02
UX / Technical Audit

Conduct a structured review of the existing site. Evaluate navigation, content hierarchy, calls to action, page load speed, and mobile responsiveness.

Test across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Run accessibility checks against WCAG guidelines to identify barriers affecting usability or conversion.

03
Brand & UI Consistency

Assess whether the site follows a consistent visual and interaction system. If a formal design system does not exist, document current typography, colors, spacing, and components.

Identify inconsistencies that create confusion or weaken brand clarity.

04
Competitive Benchmarking

Analyze successful hospitality websites to understand common patterns in booking flows, page structure, messaging, and trust signals.

Document effective approaches used by competitors or leaders in the space to identify clear opportunities for improvement.

05
Analytics & Conversion Tracking

Review existing analytics implementation and ensure key conversion events are tracked — booking completions, search submissions, contact forms, and calls to action.

Verify that Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager are configured to measure baseline performance before recommending any changes.
