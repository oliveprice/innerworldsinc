01
Client Discovery

First I must schedule a meeting with the client, AZ Mountain Inn, to establish shared understanding before any work begins. These are the questions I usually have:

—
What are you trying to do?
—
Who are your competitors?
—
What makes you different from them?
—
Why is it important?
—
If a visitor could take away one thing, what would it be?
02
UX / Technical Audit

I always conduct a structured review of the existing site. I evaluate:

—
Navigation
—
Visual hierarchy
—
Calls to action
—
Screen size responsiveness
—
Emotional engagement: does this site spark desire, curiosity, or excitement within me? Does it make me WANT to stay?

I test across desktop, tablet, and mobile. I run accessibility checks against WCAG guidelines to identify barriers affecting usability or conversion. I use accessibilitychecker.org for this.

03
Brand & UI Consistency

I then assess whether the site follows a consistent visual and interaction system. If a formal design system is NOT currently established, then I'd like to establish one by documenting typography, colors, spacing, and other components. I create a style guide, either in Figma, or within a codebase and staged on a live webpage.

I make sure to identify inconsistencies that create confusion and/or weaken brand identity.

04
Competitive Benchmarking

Early on in this stage, I always collect inspiration. I would look at successful hospitality websites to see some different methods of booking flows, page structure, hierarchy, and messaging.

My favorite sites for this purpose are Awwwards, Behance, Dribbble, and Pinterest. I create folders within my computer to keep my inspiration organized.

I'd make note of engaging approaches used by leaders in the space to identify clear opportunities for improvement.

05
Analytics & Conversion Tracking

Do they have any analytic tools in place? I would review those to ensure key conversion events are being tracked.

I would verify that Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager are configured correctly to measure performance from this point forward.
