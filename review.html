01
Overall thoughts

Much of my critique are the things I mentioned on the Faults page:

—
The site feels improvised rather than designed.
—
There is no design system or cohesive brand identity tying the site together.
—
Typography, colors, and spacing are arbitrary.
—
Visual hierarchy is inconsistent, making it difficult to know where to focus first.
—
Calls-to-action are not prominent or really prioritized.
—
Imagery is underutilized and does not convey the atmosphere or experience of the property.
—
The overall experience is generic and lacks personality.
02
Hierarchy

The hierarchy on the page is pretty weak here, and the overall experience is flat and stale. The hero image of the cabin paired with the tagline (should) be the first thing to capture attention, but it is presented in a very muted and lackluster way. The typography does not carry enough weight to create impact or draw my eye.


Currently, the features and key sections do not have clear distinctions, so it is difficult to know what to focus on. My eye simply reads from top to bottom because that is the default path, because the design is not guiding me at all. Ideally, the hierarchy should lead visitors through the page in a way that sparks curiosity and highlights the most important elements, but my sense of excitement or intentional guidance is completely missing.

03
Attention to detail

The design decisions are strange and make me think they were not intentional. For example...



—
Why do the select menus not have any outlines yet have the same background color as the body background?
—
Why does the container extend into oblivion rather than have a max height and a scrollbar?
—
Why is the menu container so far below some selectors, and right under other selectors? Why do they behave differently?
—
Why is the vertical line that indicates the hover state of the selected item above the text rather than below it?
