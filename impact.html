Measurable Improvement

If I could only change one thing that would make the most difference, it would be a complete redesign of the hero section.


Let the imagery of the cabins and mountains speak for itself first, filling the visitor's vision with what it would FEEL like. This could be a series of shifting images or a brief, looping video, so the essence of the place is seen and felt immediately. The tagline and the call-to-action must claim their place at the center, unmissable, guiding the mind with a strong prescence. Let this section occupy the whole screen and be the very first thing the visitor encounters, so the spirit of the property is understood without explanation. In this way, the visitor no longer guesses or has to read through paragraphs... they feel the place, they inhabit it for a moment, and in that experience, desire and engagement are awakened naturally.


By establishing that presence and immersion from the first moment, the site would feel much more trustworthy. This strengthened connection would make visitors far more likely to want to engage and complete a booking.
